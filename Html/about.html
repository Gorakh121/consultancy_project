Who we are

A consultancy built on transparent counseling

GBS International Educational Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is a professional education consultancy dedicated to helping Nepali students pursue higher education in South Korea. We provide honest guidance, transparent counseling, university admission support, document preparation, visa application assistance, and pre-departure orientation.

Every student's academic background, financial situation, and career goals are different — so every pathway we recommend is built around the individual, not a template.

Our Mission

To help every student choose the right academic pathway based on their qualifications and future goals — with clear, transparent guidance and complete support from application to departure.
