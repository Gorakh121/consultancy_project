Biography

A leader committed to student success

Nisha Neupane is the Founder of G.B.S International Educational Consultancy, a Nepal-based education consultancy dedicated to helping students achieve their dreams of studying abroad. With a strong commitment to quality education, ethical counseling, and student success, she has guided many aspiring students in making informed decisions about their academic and professional futures.

Originally from Simara, Nepal, Nisha has always been passionate about education and lifelong learning. She completed her higher secondary education at St. Lawrence College, Chabahil, Kathmandu, and earned her Master of Arts (M.A.) in English from Padma Kanya Multiple Campus. She is currently pursuing an MPhil, reflecting her dedication to academic excellence and continuous professional growth.

In addition to her academic achievements, Nisha is a Yoga Instructor and YouTube educator, where she shares educational and motivational content to inspire learners beyond the classroom. She believes that education is a powerful tool for personal growth and positive social change.

She has also completed online professional training in Microsoft Excel, Microsoft PowerPoint, and English language training, enabling her to combine technical expertise with effective communication and leadership skills.

As the Founder of G.B.S International Educational Consultancy, Nisha is committed to providing transparent, reliable, and student-centered educational guidance. Her vision is to connect Nepalese students with quality international education opportunities while ensuring they receive honest advice and comprehensive support throughout their study abroad journey.

With a passion for education, leadership, and service, Nisha continues to inspire students to pursue their ambitions with confidence. She believes that success comes through integrity, perseverance, continuous learning, and a genuine desire to make a positive difference in the lives of others. Her goal is to inspire others through knowledge, integrity, and lifelong learning while creating opportunities that help individuals build brighter futures.
