How It Works

Six stamps between here and departure.

Every file moves through the same sequence, in the same order, so nothing gets missed.

Free Counselling

We assess your goals, budget, and eligibility honestly — including telling you if a route isn't a fit.

Language Training

Enrol in our Japanese or Korean classes to build the score your target visa requires.

Document Prep

Academic records, financial proof, and certificates are collected and formatted to each embassy's standard.

Test Preparation

Structured JLPT, TOPIK, or EPS-TOPIK coaching with mock tests before you sit the real exam.

Visa Filing

We file your application and coach you through the embassy or employer interview.

Pre-Departure

Orientation on culture, housing, and first-week logistics — then we see you off at the airport.
