About GBS

Your Trusted Partner in Global Mobility

For over a decade, GBS International Consultancy has guided students, professionals, and families through the process of studying, working, and settling abroad — turning ambitious plans into approved applications.

Our advisors work directly with universities, employers, and immigration authorities across 20+ countries, so every step of your journey is backed by current, accurate guidance.

  • ✓ Personalized counselling for every applicant
  • ✓ Direct university and employer partnerships
  • ✓ End-to-end visa and documentation support
  • ✓ Post-arrival settlement assistance
